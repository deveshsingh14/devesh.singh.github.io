    About Me

    Back in 2021, I started my journey in Computer Science and Engineering at G.H. Raisoni College, Nagpur. Since then, I've had the privilege of building software and infrastructure in diverse environments, from crafting deep learning models for satellite data at ISRO's Space Applications Centre to driving automated CI/CD and AWS infrastructure at HotelKey.

    My main focus these days is architecting robust CI/CD pipelines, building scalable full-stack applications with Node.js and PostgreSQL, and integrating cutting-edge LLM tooling via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to streamline DevOps operations.

    When I'm not coding or provisioning cloud resources, you can probably find me fulfilling my duties as a Senior Division Cadet in the NCC Air Wing, where I'm trained in aircraft flying and drill marching, or working on personal AI agents bridging LLMs and CI/CD operations.
